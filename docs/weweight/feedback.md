# Feedback and development

Open **Settings › Send Feedback**, choose Problem, Suggestion or Question, and describe what happened. You can add an e-mail address to receive a ticket confirmation and a reply. **Add app and device details** is optional; the details are displayed before sending and do not include measurements, goals or GitHub settings. Sending needs a network connection.

Do not include weights or other health details in a report. **Write E-Mail Instead** opens an e-mail to [apps@cblum.ch](mailto:apps@cblum.ch).

[Open WeWeight issues](https://github.com/CyrilBlum/app-feedback/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22app%3A+WeWeight%22) show reported problems and requested features. The tracker is public. Anyone can read it; posting needs a GitHub account. Do not post names, e-mail addresses, health information, repository credentials or screenshots containing personal data. Reports in German are welcome.

**Settings › Planned and in Progress** opens the tracker. **What's New** lists implemented changes, also available in the [changelog](changelog.md). A changelog entry does not confirm App Store availability.
