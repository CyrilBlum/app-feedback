# Better Baby Book

Better Baby Book keeps memories and daily logs for your children. You record milestones, photos, quotes and measurements and can turn them into a photo book or a file backup. Local use needs no sync account. With the optional family sync you share selected children with other family members.

This guide describes the current development state. Some functions may be missing in an older installed version or in the version offered in the App Store. The [changelog](changelog.md) names the version of each change.

The app's interface is in German. Labels in quotation marks are written as they appear in the app, with an English translation in brackets where it helps.

## Getting started

1. Read the medical notice on first launch.
2. Open "Profil" (Profile) and "Weiteres Kind / Baby hinzufügen" (Add another child / baby). Enter the child and save the profile. With several children, select the right child before you add entries.
3. On "Übersicht" (Overview), record a daily entry, or open "Meilensteine" (Milestones) to add a developmental step.
4. Under "Profil › Daten-Export & Sicherung › Vollständiger Daten-Export" (Profile › Data export & backup › Full data export) you create an independent backup.

If other family members should edit the same entries, set up [family sync](family-sync.md) afterwards.

!!! warning "Health and backups"
    The app is for documentation and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Growth charts and reference ages are not an assessment of your child. Check important information yourself and back up your data independently of the sync. You use the app at your own risk. Mandatory statutory rights and liability remain unaffected.

## Privacy

Children's data is first stored in the app's storage. With family sync enabled, shared child bundles are encrypted on the device. Account and family metadata are outside this encryption. Export files and feedback by e-mail are also separate from it.
