# Feedback and development

For a problem, question or feature request, use **Tools › Feedback** on iPhone or **Send Feedback…** on Mac. You can also write to [apps@cblum.ch](mailto:apps@cblum.ch). Describe what you did, what you expected and what happened. Include the app version and platform.

The form accepts a message, optional reply address and optional version/device information. It returns a ticket number. Do not add private entries, calendar subscription URLs or credentials to the message.

- [Open issues for TimeTrackingTool](https://github.com/CyrilBlum/app-feedback/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22app%3A+TimeTrackingTool%22)
- [Work in progress](https://github.com/CyrilBlum/app-feedback/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22app%3A+TimeTrackingTool%22+label%3A%22status%3A+in+progress%22)
- [Changelog](changelog.md)

The tracker is public. Do not post names, e-mail addresses, client information, time records, server addresses, tokens or screenshots containing personal data. A GitHub account is needed to open an issue. E-mail is available without one. Reports in German are welcome.
