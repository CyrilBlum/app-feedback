# Feedback and development

Open **Settings › Feedback & Development › Send Feedback** to report a problem or suggest a change. You can also write to [apps@cblum.ch](mailto:apps@cblum.ch). Include the app version, the steps that led to the problem and the result you expected.

The form accepts your message, an optional reply address and optional version/device details. Vocabulary and learning progress are excluded from the automatic details. Review your message before sending and avoid adding personal material. A successful submission returns a ticket number. E-mail is available as a fallback.

- [Open Vocab Vault issues](https://github.com/CyrilBlum/app-feedback/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22app%3A+Vocab+Vault%22)
- [Work in progress](https://github.com/CyrilBlum/app-feedback/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3A%22app%3A+Vocab+Vault%22+label%3A%22status%3A+in+progress%22)
- [Changelog](changelog.md)

The tracker is public. Do not post names, e-mail addresses, personal vocabulary, photos of people, private study material or screenshots containing personal data. Reading needs no account. Opening an issue requires a GitHub account. Reports in German are welcome.
