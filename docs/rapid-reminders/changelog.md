# Changelog

The same changes are listed in the app under About & Usage › What's New. These entries describe implemented changes and do not confirm App Store availability.

## 1.1 (2026-11-03)

### New

- **Lists that keep up.** Lists with colour and symbol, items with notes, links, people, tags and dates, and categories for long lists. Ticking an item hides it; typing the same title again brings it back.
- **Smart lists.** See what is due today, scheduled, active or completed across all lists.
- **Optional account and sync.** Sign up to sync your lists; changes made offline are sent later. Without an account everything stays on this iPhone.
- **Shared lists.** Share a list with another user as editor or read-only. Participants can leave a shared list.
- **Calendar subscription.** Subscribe to one, several or all lists in your calendar app. You can renew the link, and the old one stops working.
- **Delete your account in the app.** Delete the account after confirming your password, and choose whether to keep the lists on this iPhone.
- **What's New.** This list of changes, under About & Usage. After an update with new features, it opens once and shows only those.
