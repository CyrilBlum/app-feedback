# Changelog

The same changes are listed in the app under About & Usage › What's New. These entries describe implemented changes and do not confirm App Store availability.

## 1.1 (2026-11-03)

### New

- **Lists that keep up.** Lists with colour and symbol, items with notes, links, people, tags and dates, and categories for long lists. Ticking an item hides it; typing the same title again brings it back.
- **Smart lists.** See what is due today, scheduled, active or completed across all lists.
- **Optional account and sync.** Sign up to sync your lists; changes made offline are sent later. Without an account everything stays on this iPhone.
- **Shared lists.** Share a list with another user as editor or read-only. Participants can leave a shared list.
- **Calendar subscription.** Subscribe to one, several or all lists in your calendar app. You can renew the link, and the old one stops working.
- **Delete your account in the app.** Delete the account after confirming your password, and choose whether to keep the lists on this iPhone.
- **What's New.** This list of changes, under About & Usage. After an update with new features, it opens once and shows only those.
- **Feedback and development.** Under About & Usage, a short form for problems, wishes and questions goes straight to the developer and answers with a ticket number (a mail stays possible), and a link shows the public list of planned and ongoing work.
- **Saved drafts.** Reminder, list and feedback drafts stay on this device as you type. Reopen the same editor to continue after closing the app. Cancel asks before discarding changes.
- **Sync history and recovery.** About & Usage now shows server changes and earlier local versions. Restore an earlier version as a separate list. Rejected edits stay pending, with an option to edit and retry.

### Improved

- **Sync progress and control.** The dashboard shows sync progress and offers Stop Sync. Pending changes stay on the device and can be sent later.
- **Clearer controls and layouts.** Large text has more room in reminder editors and shared lists. Calendar and shortcut choices stay selected during the session. Help and Home Screen shortcuts are together under About & Usage. Foreground colours adapt for readable contrast in light and dark mode.

### Fixed

- **Easier to tick reminders.** Checkboxes have a larger touch area, making reminders easier to complete.
- **Safer sync and sharing search.** Session changes no longer accept outdated sync responses. Searches ignore old results and show failures. Rejected edits and changes made during an upload are kept.
- **Smoother swipe actions and form interaction.** Swipe-to-delete keeps rows visible until confirmed. Forms can be scrolled to dismiss the keyboard, copying feedback contact info confirms visibly, and an app privacy manifest is included.
- **Secure session storage in Keychain.** Account session tokens are stored securely in the device Keychain instead of UserDefaults, with automatic migration from existing logins.
