# Rapid Reminders

Reusable checklists with categories, tags and optional dates. Tick an item to hide it, then type its title again when you need it next time. Local lists work without an account. An optional account adds synchronization, shared lists and calendar subscriptions.

## Quick start

1. Finish or skip the walkthrough. You can reopen it with the walkthrough button on the main screen.
2. Choose **Add List**, enter a name, choose a colour and symbol, then choose **Create**.
3. Open the list and type into **New Reminder**. Use the add button or the keyboard to submit it.
4. Tap an item to open **Details** and add notes, a date, tags or other information.
5. Tick its checkbox when done. Use the list menu's **Show Completed Items** to see it again, or enter the same title to reactivate it.

You can keep using the app locally. Read [Sync, sharing and data](sync-and-data.md) before signing in or subscribing to a calendar feed.

!!! warning "Important dates and information"
    Verify important deadlines independently and keep separate copies of important information. Sync is not a backup. Calendar refresh and alarms can be delayed or unavailable. Do not use the app as your only safeguard for important deadlines, emergencies or medical care.
